About 3D Maze

3D Maze

Windows 95 Screen Saver — web remake

A little brick corridor that wanders off on its own. It is a raycast maze, a handful of ClassicCraft block textures, and absolutely no reason to exist.

  • Double-click 3D Maze to start.
  • Move the mouse, click, or press any key to exit.
  • Right here in the Start menu: settings, preview, shut down.

Block textures from ClassicCraft by Heath Logan Campbell (MIT licence).
